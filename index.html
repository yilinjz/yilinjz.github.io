Yilin Zhang

Software Engineer at Google | M.S. at CMU LTI

I am an NLP + HCI researcher. I study how people and language-model agents come to understand each other. Most recently, I work on:

  • Human-centered agent evaluation. Does an agent serve the specific person in front of it, whose needs are often unstated and shift once they see the options? More broadly, how do AI systems change the work and decisions of the people who use them?
  • Communication between humans and multi-agent systems. How a person, or a team of people, conveys goals to a group of agents, and how the agents surface trade-offs and hidden needs back to them.

I finished my master's at CMU LTI in December 2025, advised by Prof. Sherry Tongshuang Wu. I now work at Google as a software engineer. I also collaborate with Prof. Daphne Ippolito at People + AI Research (PAIR), who advises my ongoing work on how language models reason about what individual people think and want. Before that I studied computer science at the University of Waterloo and worked with Prof. Jian Zhao on human–computer interaction.

  • Under review preliminary version: NeurIPS 2026 BiAlign Workshop, oral
    How Should Planning Agents ReSHAPE Their Search Space?

    Yilin Zhang, Xinran Zhao, Tong Chen, Ziming Yuan, Tongshuang Wu

    People rarely know what they would accept until they see the options, so a user's first request often rules out plans they would have liked. We formalize the step where an agent revises the request before planning, and show it matters more than the planner itself.

  • COLM 2026
    Penalizing Length: Uncovering Systematic Bias in Quality Estimation Metrics

    Yilin Zhang, Wenda Xu, Zhongtao Liu, Tetsuji Nakagawa, Markus Freitag

    Learned quality-estimation metrics for machine translation systematically prefer shorter translations; even error-free longer ones get marked down. We trace the cause to skewed training data and show two fixes.

    cAST: Enhancing Code Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Structural Chunking via Abstract Syntax Tree

    Yilin Zhang, Xinran Zhao, Zora Zhiruo Wang, Chenyang Yang, Jiayi Wei, Tongshuang Wu
